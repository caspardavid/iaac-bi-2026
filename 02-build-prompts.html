Build sprint · copy, adapt, paste

Prompts for your builder

Paste into your site builder (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Framer…) or your AI assistant. Replace everything in [brackets].

Starting prompt

Build a one-page website for [name]. Audience: [target customer]. Their problem, in their words: [problem]. Value proposition: [your sentence from yesterday].

Sections: a hero with headline and sub-headline; three benefits; a visual placeholder for [render / mock-up]; how it works in three steps; one call to action, "[Join the waiting list / Book a pilot call / Pre-order / Request a letter of intent]", with a form asking for [name, email, organisation, role].

Tone: plain and professional. No pricing yet. Mobile-friendly.

Then, one at a time

Give me 3 alternative headlines, each under 10 words.
Connect the form so every sign-up lands in a spreadsheet I can count.
Add a short line after sign-up saying this is an early concept and we'll be in touch.

Five habits that save you an hour

1

Context first.

Customer, problem, value proposition. Paste yesterday’s sentence.

2

Name the sections.

Say what goes on the page, and the single CTA.

3

Small steps.

One change per prompt. Big rewrites break things.

4

Ask for options.

Three headlines, then let a peer pick.

5

Stop polishing.

At 60 minutes, freeze the design and test it.

Fake doors, honestly: test demand without misleading anyone

  • Label renders and mock-ups as concepts
  • After sign-up, say it’s early and you’ll be in touch
  • Ask for a commitment you can honour: a call, a pilot, a letter of intent
  • Don’t take money for something you can’t deliver
  • If you take pre-orders, state delivery and refund terms
  • Ask before publishing anyone’s name, logo or quote
