Your market and your first unit economics

Three things, for your own project. This sheet goes in your evidence pack.

Illustrative: a daylight-analysis service for small studios. The numbers are made up to show the method. Yours need sources.

  • Start from what you know: your segment, your region, your interviews
  • Use realistic assumptions, and write down where each one comes from
  • Follow your sales process and channels: how many can you actually reach?
  • Account for resistance to change and transition time: B2B and public buyers decide slowly
  • Build scenarios: pessimistic, mid, optimistic

TAM is everyone who could have the problem; SAM the segment your product and channels can actually serve; SOM the share you can realistically win in the next 3–5 years. Investors care most about the smallest circle, and how you’ll get it.

2 · Unit economics

CAC

= sales and marketing spend in a period ÷ new customers in that period. Include people’s time, not just ads. Different channels have different CACs.

CLV

= revenue per period × gross margin × lifetime. Lifetime ≈ 1 ÷ churn; churn = 1 − retention.

CLV ÷ CAC

above 3× healthy
1–3× you survive, barely
below 1× every new customer loses money

Payback

= CAC ÷ monthly margin. Aim for under 12 months.

€150/month at 70% gross margin = €105 margin per month; 3% leave each month → lifetime ≈ 33 months → CLV ≈ €3,500. With CAC €600: 5.8×, payback ≈ 6 months.

No subscription? Many built-environment businesses sell projects. CLV = margin per project × repeat projects. Productise what you can (fixed scope, fixed price). Recurring revenue beats one-off: maintenance, monitoring, licences, memberships. Long sales cycles raise CAC: count the months, not just the euros.

3 · How much money you need

Plan 12–18 months, in three scenarios: pessimistic, mid, optimistic. Include salaries (yours too), material and production costs, revenues (starting later than you think), and other expenses: tools, legal, travel, certification.

Runway = cash in the bank ÷ monthly net burn.
