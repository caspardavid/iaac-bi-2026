13–29 Nov · no classes, real customers

The validation sprint

Run the plan you wrote yesterday, with the artefact you built today.

Minimum by 29 Nov

5+

Interviews.

With target customers, not friends. 8 if you’re a pair. Recorded and synthesised.

1

Live test.

Today’s artefact, live and measured against the threshold you set.

1+

Traction evidence.

A letter of intent, a customer endorsement, or pre-subscriptions.

Traction you can collect by 30 Nov

Letters of intent (B2B).

Usually non-binding. Shows active interest. Signed by a qualified customer. Aim for 4–6.

Endorsements (B2B · B2C).

On video or with a photo. At least 3. Target customers, not random people. Ask permission to publish.

Pre-subscriptions (B2B · B2C).

A list of people ready to buy, from your landing page or in person. Photograph your interviews and tests.

A suggested rhythm

13–19 NovBook and run interviews. Share your link. Fix what confuses people.
20–26 NovIterate the offer. Ask for commitments: LOIs, pilots, pre-orders.
27–29 NovSynthesise. Update the canvas and your numbers. Build the pitch.
30 NovDeck uploaded by 12:00. Pitch at 14:30.

No check-in is planned during the sprint. Questions go through the channel announced in class.

Interview prompts

From yesterday.

1 · Check my guide

Here is my interview guide for [customer] about [problem]: [paste]. Flag every leading question, every question about the future or about my idea, and rewrite each one to ask about past behaviour.

2 · Synthesise

Here is an interview transcript: [paste]. Extract: the problem in their words (exact quotes), what they do today, what it costs them, signs of urgency or budget, and any commitment they offered. Then say whether it supports or contradicts this assumption: [assumption]. Quote only what is in the transcript.

3 · Devil's advocate

These are my assumptions and my evidence so far: [paste]. Act as a sceptical investor. Which assumption is weakest? What result next week would prove me wrong?

Ask permission before recording, and remove names and anything sensitive before uploading. AI summaries flatten nuance and sometimes invent quotes: check every quote in your evidence pack against the transcript.

Four things to remember

Productise

whenever you can: fixed scope, fixed price.

Recurring beats one-off

revenue that comes back every month is worth more.

The market is key

a good team in a bad market loses to the market.

Talk to customers

every week. Not just until 29 Nov.

Go and test it. See you on 30 Nov.
