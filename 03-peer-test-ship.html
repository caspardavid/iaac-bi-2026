Build sprint · 10:45–11:15

Peer test, then ship

Peer test · 10:45–11:00 · the 5-second test

Pair up with another project. Show your page for five seconds, then hide it and ask:

  1. What is it?
  2. Who is it for?
  3. What do I do next?
If the answers don’t match your headline and your CTA, fix those first.

Ship · 11:00–11:15

  1. Publish
  2. Check the form works: submit a test sign-up yourself and see that it arrives
  3. Post the link in the shared folder

Done means

Your threshold is the number you set yesterday in your validation plan, for example “≥ 2 pilot calls booked”.
