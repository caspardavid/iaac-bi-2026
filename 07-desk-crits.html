Desk crits · 17:00–18:15 · 75 min

One-minute dry run

In groups. One minute to pitch, four minutes of feedback. The teacher joins each group in turn.

Your one minute

  1. Problem
  2. Customer
  3. Solution
  4. What you’ll test

After your turn: write down the one thing you’ll change.

Feedback questions for peers

  1. Was the problem clear in 10 seconds?
  2. Is the customer specific?
  3. Is there evidence, or only claims?
  4. What’s the ask?
  5. What would you cut?
