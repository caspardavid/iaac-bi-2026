Illustrative. A clear “we were wrong, so we changed X” scores as well as a confirmation. A slide with no numbers scores badly.

Pitch tips

  • Open with the problem, not your name or your tech
  • Numbers beat adjectives: “6 of 8” beats “very positive feedback”
  • Show a real customer’s words
  • Rehearse with a timer. Four minutes means four
  • End with a specific ask: money, a pilot site, an introduction

Templates to look at: Sequoia Capital’s pitch outline, Airbnb’s 2008 deck.

Two different documents

Pitch deck · 30 Nov.

Supports a talk: you are the content, the slides help. Few slides, little text: one idea per slide, big numbers. Goal: attention, make them want the next conversation.

Extended deck · 7 Dec.

Read without you: self-explanatory, complete sentences. With an appendix: interview notes, test results, traction, calculations, sources. Goal: trust, every claim can be checked.
